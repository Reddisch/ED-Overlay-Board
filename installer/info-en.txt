ED Overlay Board – movable VR overlay windows for Elite Dangerous

Note: This program was created with the help of AI (Claude by Anthropic).
It is an unofficial fan tool and is not affiliated with Frontier Developments.

What gets installed?
- The companion app (EDOverlayBoard.exe); it runs in the notification area.
- An OpenXR API layer (EDOverlayBoard_Layer.dll). It is registered for your
  Windows user and loaded into OpenXR games by the OpenXR loader. It only
  shows something while the app is running.

No administrator rights are needed. Uninstalling removes the layer
registration; your config.json and your images are kept.

Requirements: Windows 10 (2004+) / 11, an OpenXR runtime (e.g. Virtual
Desktop VDXR, SteamVR, Meta) and Elite Dangerous running through OpenXR
(e.g. with OpenComposite).
